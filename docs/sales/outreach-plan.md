# Contact & outreach plan

Sales PLAN item 4 (`docs/sales/PLAN.md`): who EDMA's engagement team approaches, in what
order, through which channel, with which materials, saying what. Companion documents: the
master narrative (`master-narrative.md`), the numbers (`benefits-one-sheet.md`), and the
per-organisation one-pagers (`one-pagers/`). Objection handling is grounded in
`docs/research/prior-art.md`.

**Rules (binding, from PLAN.md + decision 0002 R1–R8):**

- **Public roles/offices only.** Targets below are offices and public divisional structures,
  never scraped personal data. Where a specific office title is web-verifiable it is cited;
  otherwise the generic office is named and marked *(generic)*. **Verify all names/roles at
  send time** — this plan names no private individuals, and the EDMA team should address
  people only through the org's own published structure or the EDMA relationship.
- **The EDMA-internal route is always preferred** over cold contact: member rep, DEP
  relationship, or CoP convener intro.
- **No offer language** (decision 0002, R1): every email states concept-demo status and that
  nothing is an offer or solicitation of credit. All demo figures are illustrative (R5).
- **Mastercard caveat travels everywhere**: its membership comes from the internal role-map
  sheet (4 Jun 2026) and is publicly unconfirmed (research-gaps C4) — re-verify with EDMA
  before any Mastercard outreach or member-based claim.
- Every claim in outreach copy is cited to `docs/research/` — no unverified numbers.

## 4. Objection handling — Q&A

The three prior-art sentences (`docs/research/prior-art.md` §9) expanded for live use. The
differentiation table in prior-art §9 is the leave-behind for any engineer who wants the full
comparison.

**Q1. "Who else has done this?"**
Everyone has done a piece; nobody has assembled it. The GSEs proved verified digital data
saves up to 12 days and grants rep-and-warrant relief — the strongest institutional
endorsement of verified-data rails, and the incumbent rail the showcase re-plumbs (prior-art
§2). FormFree — a GSE-approved verification vendor — proved a mortgage vendor will issue
borrower-held VCs (prior-art §3). Flanders' Athumi runs production Solid pods at government
scale, with a bank among its use-case partners (prior-art §8). GLEIF and the BIS proved
reusable organisational credentials cut repeated onboarding (prior-art §5). And the EU is
legislating wallet acceptance into every bank by end-2026 (prior-art §7). What none of them
combined is a user-held general data store + cross-party credential reuse + zero-knowledge
disclosure + receipted consent — that combination is this showcase (prior-art, header).

**Q2. "Didn't VC/blockchain pilots fail?"**
The documented failures were horizontal platforms, token projects, and ledgers with no
consumer. Trinsic's founder's own post-mortem names the cause: no anchor vertical, no use-case
concentration (prior-art §4). Bloom was token-financed and ended in an SEC settlement
(prior-art §4). The land-registry DLT pilots (HMLR, Lantmäteriet) were ledger-centric with
nothing consumer-held — and notably, HMLR's non-DLT digital-deed slice *did* ship (prior-art
§6). None combined an anchor vertical, a user-held store, and privacy tech — the land-registry
pilots had the vertical but nothing consumer-held; the platforms had the technology but no
vertical — we start from all three. Mortgage supplies exactly what the failures lacked: an
$11,600-per-loan cost problem,
existing GSE-approved verifier roles to map issuers onto, and regulation pushing wallet
acceptance (prior-art §4, lesson).

**Q3. "Is this real technology or a research toy?"**
Solid pods run in production in Flanders (prior-art §8); vLEIs are commercially issued
(prior-art §5); W3C VC 2.0 is a ratified standard (prior-art §3); our ZK stack proves live
in-browser (prior-art §9). The only thing unproven is the combination — which is exactly what
a showcase is for. And where the demo simulates, it says so on screen: watermarked API
payloads, an honesty panel on every proving surface, and a "what is simulated" appendix in the
narrative (master narrative §6).

**Q4. "Why not Microsoft Entra Verified ID — or just wait for the EUDI wallet?"**
Two different answers. *Entra*: the showcase's credentials must be pod-resident,
RDF-canonicalised, and carry the ZK-friendly signatures the proving stack verifies; Entra
Verified ID cannot produce those, so it was assessed and dropped from the architecture —
narrative-only (decision 0004). (If Microsoft comes up: CDMC *workgroup participant*, never
"member" — research-gaps §3.) *EUDI*: for European members it is the tailwind, not the
alternative — eIDAS 2.0 forces wallet acceptance at every EU bank by end-2026, but the wallet
is attestation-only: there is no general data store for the loan file to live in, selective
disclosure is still unsettled in the ARF, and mortgage remains a slideware use case with no
dedicated pilot (prior-art §7). The showcase is the EUDI mortgage story built out further —
store + ZK + receipts, today — before their competitors get there.

**Q5. "How mature is the zero-knowledge stack — would security sign off?"**
Lead with the honesty panel; it is repeated verbatim on every proving surface (master
narrative §6; decision 0005): the proving stack is **research-grade** — internally
re-audited, external cryptographer sign-off still pending; a passing proof is not a
production guarantee. The honest claim is "the value is not disclosed and cannot be forged"
— where "cannot be forged" means the stack verifies issuer-bound proofs under its documented
cryptographic assumptions, subject to that pending external audit — *not* "the verifier
cannot possibly learn it": for low-entropy values (a credit score has 551 possible values) a
determined verifier could enumerate candidates. Some composed proofs
are precomputed offline and verified live; the screen labels them "proved offline · verified
live", and a live proof only counts alongside its issuer-signed anchor credential. Every
partner one-pager already gates ZK productionisation on external audit. On the broader
security question: receipted access reduces unnecessary standing copies — the breach surface
that produced 14.7M-person and 885M-document exposures [38][39] — but the honest claim is
scoped, enumerable, revocable *authorizations*, not zero copies; regulated parties still
retain what retention rules require, and the pod host stays inside the trust boundary until
host-blind custody is built (master narrative §6; `one-pagers/verizon.md`).

**Q6. "What does it cost to pilot?"**
No priced offer exists or may exist at this stage (decision 0002, R1) — commercial terms are
a partner-phase conversation with EDMA [EDMA-TODO]. What keeps pilot scope deliberately thin:
each one-pager's pilot is an increment beside existing rails, not a platform build — an
issuance endpoint on top of an existing verification product (Equifax, Mastercard), a thin
verify-and-quote surface (Fifth Third), pod delivery beside the existing eVault flow (Wolters
Kluwer), one loan file's verifications (Freddie Mac). The reference implementation, the
FIBO↔MISMO binding table, and the CDMC conformance frame are CoP deliverables the partner
consumes rather than funds (master narrative §3/§5). Per-role scope and effort bands arrive
with the product-proposal template (PLAN item 7) for each org that engages.
