## The proposition

Title has no EDMA member — an open seat (org-map, gaps). The industry's standing-copy risk is
one you know at first hand: the 2019 exposure of 885 million title documents (and the $1M
NYDFS penalty) [39] is precisely the class of risk that shrinks when parties hold scoped,
receipted access instead of durable copies (mortgage-process §4). A title-clear credential
issued once into the loan file gives lender, settlement agent, and investor the same
attestation without another copy of the underlying search — and puts the segment's
data-and-technology leader in the standards conversation early.

## What building it for real entails

- A title-clear credential issuer summarising commitment status — the full ALTA commitment
  (A/B-I/B-II schedules) as structured credential data is the product-phase step [17][18].
- Settlement integration: the closing app consumes the title credential as a pre-close
  condition (SPEC §2), with Endpoint the natural pilot surface (org-map role 7).
- Freshness/status semantics — commitments carry effective dates and requirements that clear
  over time (decision 0007).
- Honest retention design: title plants and policy records you must keep are kept; the claim
  is fewer *unmanaged* copies downstream, with authorizations receipted (master narrative §6).
